CyberSparring · Project information

About CyberSparring

CyberSparring is a project in development for collaborative, hands-on cybersecurity learning. Its goal is to help people practise technical skills, exchange experience and learn from each other in a supportive environment.

The initial training format pairs a Blue Team participant, who configures and hardens a lab server, with a Red Team participant, who performs authorized penetration tests within an agreed scope. They work independently first, then review evidence, report findings and discuss improvements together.

How does a CyberSparring session work?

Prepare an isolated lab, configure and harden a system, test it with permission, document findings, then jointly review the results and improve the configuration. The focus is on learning and feedback, not defeating another participant.

Is the matching platform available?

Not yet. Partner matching and shared practice environments are ideas for possible future development. For now, CyberSparring presents an educational concept and a practical approach to cybersecurity exercises.
